Hypothesis: image-bearing requests are delayed by the direct IPv6 upload path. Passive owned-PID evidence during iteration-05 showed queued upload bytes, repeated TCP retransmissions and low congestion windows. Text-only requests took about 3–4 seconds; later requests replayed 1.35–4.20 MB of PNG data. These observations support an upload bottleneck but do not identify address family causally.
