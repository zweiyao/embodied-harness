# Final hypothesis assessment
H1 — Confirmed contributing mechanism: decision-budget consumed by retrieval/localization and repeated ineffective actions. Not a unique universal cause: 40/47 failures exhausted 30 model requests; median elapsed time 219.55s; representative cases have 0 actions after 19 reads, or 15 move_pose calls with gripper always open. Flash non-action tool calls 1385/1938 vs Astra 717/1492. First physical-action request median 12 among Flash episodes that acted. These are observational contributions, not causal attribution percentages.
H2a — Confirmed interface misuse: 22 back_project errors (12 schema-name errors, four out-of-bounds, five empty filtered regions, one malformed range). Segment has eight argument errors among 26 errors; remaining errors include detection failures. Errors cannot explain all outcomes: 21 failed episodes have no tool-return error.
H2b — Unsupported systematic coordinate arithmetic defect: replayed all 615 successful back_project returns, 382 point and 233 region, exactly matching saved maps/declared reductions. Reprojection median 0.151px/max3.058px. Does not prove correct visual target choice, calibrated physical accuracy, or collision-free grasp/place pose.
H2c — Surviving visual grounding/region selection concern: 53 region estimates differ by >5cm between center and median; broad regions can mix background. No ground-truth object-surface labels used, so incorrect target choice rates remain unknown.
H3 — Confirmed execution/feedback problem in specific traces; not isolated controller defect: Flash 59/394 move calls end >3cm from target; Astra86/483 also do. Repeated adjustments without useful recovery observed. Exact collision/IK/contact cause not resolved by scalar distance alone.
H4 — Time waiting contributes to 7 final timeouts and original 600s cohort, but cannot explain 40 interaction-budget failures. Earlier 38.1-second wait statistic belongs only to the original600s cohort and must not be generalized to later runs.
